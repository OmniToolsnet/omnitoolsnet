# OmniTools.net — 300+ Free Online Tools, Calculators & Converters

**Official Website:** [https://omnitools.net/](https://omnitools.net/)

OmniTools is a free, fast, browser-based utility suite offering **368+ client-side tools** across finance, PDF & document editing, media conversion, education, SEO, and software development — with **zero signups** and **100% privacy** (files and inputs stay inside your browser).

---

## Core Tool Categories

- **Finance & Tax Calculators:** [GST Calculator](https://omnitools.net/tools/gst-calculator), [UK VAT Calculator](https://omnitools.net/tools/uk-vat-calculator), [FIRE Calculator](https://omnitools.net/tools/fire-calculator), [SIP Calculator](https://omnitools.net/tools/sip-calculator), [Loan Balance Calculator](https://omnitools.net/tools/loan-balance-calculator)
- **PDF & Image Utilities:** [Bulk Image Compressor](https://omnitools.net/tools/image-compressor), PDF Converters, Resizers, and Formatters
- **Text, SEO & Productivity:** [Word & Character Counter](https://omnitools.net/tools/word-counter), [Fancy Font Generator](https://omnitools.net/tools/fancy-font-generator), ATS Resume Tools
- **Developer Utilities:** JSON/CSV Formatters, Regex Helpers, Encoders & Generators

Explore all tools: [https://omnitools.net/tools](https://omnitools.net/tools)
